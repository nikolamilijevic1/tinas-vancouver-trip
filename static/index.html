WHILE YOU'RE IN VANCOUVER

Three coffee & food walks

Scenic 6 to 11 km routes with specialty coffee, warm bakeries, and waterfront or neighborhood views along the way.

8.5 km · ~2 hrs · Waterfront & Pastries

False Creek Seawall to Kitsilano

A flat, breezy waterfront walk from Yaletown across to Granville Island and along the beaches into Kitsilano, paced with some of the city's best bakeries.

Open route in Google Maps →
  1. Small Victory Bakery Yaletown

    Start with a flat white and a warm croissant or sourdough toast.

  2. False Creek Seawall & Granville Island Waterfront

    Walk the seawall under the bridges to the Public Market (worth a stop at Lee's Donuts).

  3. Beaucoup Bakery Fir St

    Quick detour up Fir Street for flaky French pastries and seasonal tarts.

  4. Kits Beach to Their There W 4th Ave

    Stroll past Vanier Park and Kitsilano Beach, then finish with a latte and mochi donut.

6.5 km · ~1.5 hrs · Roasters & Neighborhoods

Gastown to Mount Pleasant Coffee Crawl

Cobblestone alleys, architectural coffee bars, and Main Street's indie shops — an easy afternoon loop through Vancouver's standout specialty roasters.

Open route in Google Maps →
  1. Revolver Coffee Gastown

    Minimalist brick-and-wood bar pouring rotating single-origin roasters from around the world.

  2. Prototype Coffee East Van

    Small-batch micro-roaster known for careful pour-overs and unusual single origins.

  3. Nemesis Coffee Great Northern Way

    Walk toward the striking circular timber pavilion for in-house roasted coffee and standout pastries.

  4. Elysian Coffee Mount Pleasant

    Finish around Ontario & Main Street near local boutiques, bookstores, and neighborhood murals.

10.5 km · ~2.5 hrs · Ocean & Cedars

Stanley Park Seawall & West End

The classic Vancouver coastal loop — towering old-growth cedars, North Shore mountain views, Lions Gate Bridge, and a cozy neighborhood cafe at the end.

Open route in Google Maps →
  1. Beaucoup (St. Regis) → Coal Harbour Downtown

    Grab a hot coffee and pastry to go, then drop down to the Coal Harbour waterfront path.

  2. Brockton Point to Lions Gate Bridge Stanley Park

    Follow the seawall around the lighthouse with wide-open harbour and mountain views.

  3. Siwash Rock & Third Beach West Side

    Quieter Pacific side of the loop — crisp ocean air and towering evergreens.

  4. English Bay to Greenhorn Cafe West End

    Step off the seawall onto tree-lined Nicola Street for a warm espresso and late brunch.
